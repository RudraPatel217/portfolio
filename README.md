# Portfolio Workspace

Place your assets in the following directories:
- **Resume/CV**: Place PDF/DOCX into `assets/documents/` (e.g. `resume.pdf`)
- **Profile Photo**: Place your headshot into `assets/images/` (e.g. `photo.png` or `avatar.jpg`)
- **Data**: Review or update `data/profile.json` (auto-extracted from your hackathons)
